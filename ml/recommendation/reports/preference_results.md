# PreferenceScore results (plan 10) - simulated data

Generated by `python -m recsys.evaluate` from `data/sim/dataset` (catalog `seed`, 60 products, train seed 42). **All numbers are on simulated data.**

## Protocol

- Test = full ranking: per test user, every catalog product they have not interacted with in train/val (`eval_candidates`), ties broken by `product_id`. `R_u` = the held-out last product (label >= 1); every test user is included. No sampled metric.
- Each user has exactly one held-out product (`|R_u| = 1`), so **Recall@K = HitRate@K** and **Precision@K = HitRate@K / K**; the graded gain cancels in NDCG.
- 95% CI = percentile interval of 1000 bootstrap resamples of users (seed 42). `coverage@10` = share of the catalog in at least one top-10 (no CI); `mean_price@10` = mean `p_price` of the top 10.
- Hyperparameters, the best model and the calibration were chosen on **val** only, with the **sampled** val-group NDCG@10 (1 positive + 20 negatives). That number is optimistic and used only for selection; the test numbers below are the final ones and were used for nothing.
- Popularity is pushed down on val because half of the val negatives are popularity-sampled.
- PreferenceScore = sigmoid (Platt) of the raw model score, fitted on val. Its probabilities are relative to the sampled 1:20 negative ratio, not the real prevalence. No feature comes from the face-shape model.

Best model (highest val NDCG@10 among LR / RF / XGBoost binary / XGBoost ranker): **XGBoost ranker**.
